## Listening Part 1 expansion (sets 002–010)

The 54 expansion photographs were generated one asset at a time with the
`photorealistic-natural` mode. Every final prompt used this production frame:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: TOEIC-format Listening Part 1
> test photograph. Primary request: [description below]. Scene/backdrop: a
> realistic location specific to the action. Style/medium: photorealistic
> candid editorial photograph. Composition/framing: landscape, medium-wide
> eye-level view, one unambiguous action or state, all key objects visible.
> Lighting/mood: neutral realistic lighting, natural textures. Constraints:
> match the request exactly; no readable text, logos, or watermark. Avoid:
> the three distractor actions for that item and a staged advertising look.
